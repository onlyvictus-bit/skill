# Core method

Use this reference for adaptive depth, evidence handling, consequential decisions, authority resolution, and contradiction routing.

## Adaptive depth

- **Depth 0 — trivial:** one tiny reversible edit, no new behavior, no research or architectural choice. One direct verification.
- **Depth 1 — standard:** bounded reversible task with settled authority. Inspect relevant sources, define done, plan the local change, verify target plus surrounding health.
- **Depth 2 — consequential:** architecture/public contract, multi-component integration, money/trading, privacy/security, concurrency, migration, or hard-to-reverse choice. Require explicit requirements/criteria, alternative mechanisms, dirty-worktree protection, approval digests, cheapest falsifier, runtime observation, and adversarial review.
- **Depth 3 — campaign/high risk:** multi-milestone or long-running work with interruption/recovery, multiple systems, material external consequences, or high blast radius. Add persistent state, milestone approvals, recovery gates, evidence lineage, and broader regression checks.

Escalate depth when new evidence raises risk. Do not lower depth merely to make a deadline easier.

## Evidence classes

Keep these separate:

- **Facts:** opened authoritative sources or explicit approved requirements.
- **Observations:** command, test, runtime, measurement, render, or data output actually observed.
- **Assumptions:** unproven conditions needed by a plan.
- **Inferences:** conclusions drawn from facts/observations; state the support.
- **Unknowns:** material unanswered questions; name the cheapest way to learn them.

Correlated evidence sharing one dataset, document, fixture, model output, or downstream copy is one evidence family. Never turn repetition into independence.

## Consequential decision synthesis

When the mechanism matters, delegate the reasoning core to `deep-plan` if available while keeping Fable as governor. Compare materially different mechanisms:

1. no-build/minimal change;
2. simplest technically complete solution;
3. stronger hybrid or more resilient alternative.

A viable recommendation states why it fits the evidence and constraints, why serious alternatives lost, its weakest assumption/evidence family, the cheapest falsifying test, a pivot condition, and unresolved material questions. Prefer the smallest technically complete option.

## Authority and contradictions

Within applicable system/developer instructions, platform policy, tool permissions, and explicit authorization boundaries, resolve project-level authority in this order:

1. explicit current user instruction;
2. approved project specification/requirements;
3. primary evidence and official documentation;
4. tests;
5. current behavior;
6. external advice;
7. memory.

A contradiction is not noise. If code, test, spec, runtime, or user instruction disagree materially, stop the affected action, preserve the evidence, resolve authority, and update requirements/plan if needed. A changed plan invalidates old approval and evidence basis.

## Research discipline

Research current sources when a decision depends on current APIs, standards, products, prices, regulations, or external behavior. Prefer official/primary sources. Separate source date from event/data date. Stop after the decision has enough evidence; two consecutive lookups that add nothing new are a strong stopping signal.
